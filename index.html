ABOUT

Research grounded in enterprise practice

Pawan Kumar Singh is an independent researcher and enterprise solutions architect with more than two decades of experience across ERP, supply chain, procurement, warehouse management, real estate/IWMS, cloud integration, APIs, and enterprise transformation.

His current work focuses on practical design-science evaluation of governed enterprise AI: where AI should interpret, where deterministic controls should intervene, when humans should exercise judgment, and how authoritative systems should preserve transaction integrity.
